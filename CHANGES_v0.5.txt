CWMS v0.5 — Administration & Withdrawal Refunds

Includes all v0.4 usability corrections, plus:
- Settings renamed to Settings & Administration.
- Settings controls for monthly contribution, registration fee, loan interest %, repayment months, financial year, opening cash, contribution due day and report title.
- Optional switches for registration fees in fund cash and outstanding interest in Total Fund Value.
- New Member Withdrawal & Contribution Refund transaction area under Settings.
- Refunds preserve original contribution history, reduce cash/fund value, show separately on Member Statement and Monthly Statement, and create Audit Log entries.
- Recording a refund automatically marks the member Withdrawn.
- Member Statement shows Gross Contributions, Contribution Refunds and Net Contributions Retained.
- Monthly Statement includes refunds in Money Out and cash reconciliation.
- Dashboard shows Gross Contributions and refunded amount separately.
- Withdrawn members stop accruing expected contributions after their refund/withdrawal month.
- Welfare remains blank unless a new welfare entry is deliberately recorded.
