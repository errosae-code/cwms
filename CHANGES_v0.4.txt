CWMS v0.4 — Verification & Usability Corrections

Implemented:
- Members display in existing membership-number ascending order; no renumbering.
- Member Add form hidden behind + Add New Member modal.
- Member Edit and safe Delete actions (members with financial history cannot be deleted; use status instead).
- Contributions display one selected month at a time; previous months remain in reports/statements.
- Contribution Add/Edit/Delete actions with Audit Log entries.
- Contribution entry form hidden behind + Add Contribution modal.
- Loans now show Issue Date and Due Date.
- Loan Add/Edit/Delete actions; loans with repayments cannot be deleted.
- Issue Loan form hidden behind + Issue Loan modal.
- Repayment Add/Edit/Delete actions; loan balances are recalculated after edits/deletes.
- Repayment form hidden behind + Add Repayment modal.
- Welfare entry form hidden behind + Add Welfare Entry modal.
- Member Statement marks future months as Not Yet Due.
- Monthly Statement contribution status supports Paid / Partial / Unpaid / Not Yet Due and sorts members by number.
- Monthly Statement includes a cash reconciliation line.
- Dashboard Active Loans counts all loans with an outstanding balance, with overdue shown separately.
- Loan interest setting display corrected to 10% format and new-loan calculation handles percentage storage safely.
- Audit logging expanded for edits/deletes.

Database corrections already applied separately in Supabase:
- Loan interest setting normalized to 0.10 (10%).
- Existing provisional welfare entry soft-deleted so Welfare is blank for now.
- Migration/correction Audit Log entries added.

Safety:
- Existing member numbers are retained.
- Existing contributions, loans and repayments are not renumbered or re-imported.
- Financial deletes use soft-delete where applicable and are logged.
